How it works

Three simple steps

No complex systems, no integrations and no work on your side. We do the preparation - you collect the bookings.

1

You share a list

A simple list of past customers your business is permitted to contact - name and phone, nothing extra.

2

We write the messages

Win-back messages tailored to your business, in a personal and respectful tone, with clear business identification and an opt-out in every message.

3

You collect the bookings

The messages go out from your business to your customers - and at the end of the week you see exactly how many of them came back to book.
