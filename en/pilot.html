In detail

Every day, one step forward

1

Share a list

You send us a simple list of past customers - first name and phone only. We organize it, remove duplicates and make sure everyone on it is a customer the business is permitted to contact.

2

Tailored copy

We write win-back message copy in your business's tone and pass it to you for approval before anything is sent. Nothing goes out that you haven't seen and approved.

3

Sending begins

On days 3-4 you send the messages from the business, following the send plan you received - in small waves at convenient hours, so you can answer replies in real time.

4

Replies come in

Customers reply directly to you. You book appointments with them as usual and mark in the tracking sheet who replied and who booked.

5

Gentle reminder

On days 5-6 a single second message goes out - only to people who didn't reply and didn't ask to opt out. Anyone who asked not to be contacted gets nothing, starting from the very first message.

6

Data collection

We count together with you: how many messages went out, how many customers replied, how many appointments were rebooked and how much revenue was recovered based on your appointment prices.

7

Report and decision

You receive a full, clear end-of-week report. Now you have real numbers from the field - and only if they convince you will you decide to continue.

$

Your cost

$0. We don't ask for a credit card, don't take payment details, and there's no automatic charge at the end of the week. Continuation depends on your explicit decision only.

Getting ready

Your preparation: 10 minutes

Everything needed to start the pilot week.

What we need from you

  • A list of past customers - first name and phone, in any convenient format (Excel, WhatsApp, a page).
  • A few sentences about the business - what you do, what a typical appointment costs, and the tone that suits you.
  • Availability to answer replies during working hours - customers reply directly to you.

What you'll get from us

  • Message copy approved by you - personal, respectful, with an opt-out.
  • A send plan for the week - who, when and in what order.
  • A simple tracking sheet for replies, bookings and revenue.
  • An end-of-week report with all the numbers, and a short wrap-up call.
