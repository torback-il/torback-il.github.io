What's in the report and how it's measured

Messages sent

An exact count of the messages that actually went out during the week, per the send plan and the tracking sheet.

Customers replied

Every customer who replied to the message - whether they booked or not. This metric tells you how "alive" your list is.

Appointments rebooked

The core metric: how many customers actually came back to the calendar. Only genuinely booked appointments count - not "interested".

Revenue recovered

The number of appointments that came back, multiplied by your appointment price. A simple, transparent calculation you can check yourself.

What the report lets you do

  • Decide by numbers - not by gut feeling - whether the service is worth its cost.
  • Get to know your list - which share of your past customers is still open to coming back.
  • Improve the next round - which copy worked better, and on which day replies were strongest.
We want you to know: we don't promise a specific result and we don't show "averages" - simply because we don't yet have enough pilots behind us to present a fair average. What is guaranteed: at the end of the 7 days you'll have accurate data about your business.
