The process

Three steps, in full depth

The same three steps you saw on the home page - here in enough detail to know exactly what you're signing up for.

Step 1: You share a list

Everything starts with a simple list of past customers - people who visited you before and simply never came back. A first name and a phone number is enough. No treatment history, no emails, no addresses.

Two important conditions for the list:

  • Only people who were actually your customers - we don't work with purchased lists or strangers.
  • Only customers the business is permitted to contact - meaning people who never asked not to be contacted.

Step 2: We write and plan

We learn your business - the field, the tone, your appointment prices - and prepare a full work package:

  • Personal win-back message copy, in your business's tone - not a generic template.
  • A send plan for the week: when to send, to whom, and at what pace.
  • A simple tracking sheet you fill in during the week - it becomes the basis of the final report.

Step 3: You send - and collect the bookings

Sending stays in your hands, from the business's number, to your customers. That's a core principle: the customer gets a message from the business they know, not from an outside party. When they reply - they reply directly to you, and you book the appointment as usual.

What does happen

  • A personal message using the customer's name - not "dear customer", but a named approach with real context for the business.
  • Clear identification of the business in every message - the customer knows who is contacting them.
  • An explicit opt-out in every message - anyone who asks not to receive messages stops receiving them. Period.
  • Respectful timing - sending at reasonable hours, with no flooding.
  • Full measurement - every reply, booking and revenue figure is recorded and appears in the report.

What doesn't happen

  • We don't buy lists and don't contact people who were never your customers.
  • We don't send on your behalf - messages go out from you; we never touch your number.
  • We don't promise a specific result - we commit to accurate measurement, not to numbers.
  • We don't need access to your calendar or systems - no integration and no installations.
