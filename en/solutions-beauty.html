Your reality

Why clients don't come back on their own

  • Life comes first. Even a happy client postpones the next booking - until she forgets completely.
  • Series break midway. A client who stopped a treatment series doesn't always know she can - and should - complete it.
  • A personal approach works. A warm message using her name, from the beautician she knows - not a generic ad.
Illustrative example
Hi Sarah, this is Emma from the studio. Some time has passed since your last treatment, and a few convenient slots opened up this week. Want us to book you in?Not interested? Reply "STOP" and we won't reach out again.
