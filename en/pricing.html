Everything worth knowing about payment

Really $0 for the first week? What's the catch?
There's no catch. The pilot week costs $0, we don't ask for a credit card, and payment details are never taken at any stage of the pilot. Our model is simple: if the service works, you'll see it in the numbers and want to continue. If not - you paid nothing.
How is payment made if I choose to continue?
Only after you explicitly say you want to continue do we settle the plan (weekly or monthly) and the payment method that works for you. There's no automatic charge and no subscription that starts "by itself".
Can I stop in the middle?
Yes. You can stop at any time, with no penalties and no commitment period. The model is weekly - not punitive.
What if the pilot week doesn't bring customers back?
Then you paid nothing, and you still have something valuable: accurate data on how your past customers behave. We don't promise a specific result - we commit that you'll know exactly what happened.
Why is the model built this way?
Because a small business shouldn't have to gamble on a service it doesn't know. One measured week is worth more than any sales page - including this one.
