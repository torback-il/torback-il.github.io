About TorBack - why we built this service

A new service with one goal: to help appointment-based businesses bring back the customers who already know them - in full transparency and with no promises in the air.

The story

The problem we solve

Almost every appointment-based business sits on a quiet treasure: a list of past customers. People who came, paid, were happy - and simply never came back. Not because something went wrong, but because life happened.

The problem is that a small business owner has no time and no tools to reach them one by one. They don't sit down to write 140 personal messages, don't build a send plan, and don't track who replied and what each booking is worth.

TorBack was born exactly in that gap: we do the preparation work - copywriting, timing, measurement - and you send from your business and collect the bookings.

Why a free week, of all things?

Because we're a new service, and we know it. We don't have a wall of success stories yet - and we're not going to invent one. Instead, we have a fair offer: give us one week on us, and you'll get real data about your business. If the numbers convince you - we'll be glad to continue. If not - you walk away with new knowledge at $0.

Our principles

  • Full transparency. Accurate measurement instead of big promises. What doesn't exist - we don't invent.
  • Respect for your customers. Polite copy, an opt-out in every message, zero flooding.
  • Your business first. You send, you answer, you book - we're behind the scenes.
  • Commercial fairness. See results first, pay later. No auto-renewal and no traps.
One thing you won't find here: fabrications. This site has no invented customers, no invented testimonials, no "thousands of businesses already joined" and no brand logos. When we have real stories - we'll ask permission and tell them. Until then, the data from your own pilot is the best proof there is.
