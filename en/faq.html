Frequently asked questions - straight answers

Compliance, privacy, pricing, and what happens if it doesn't work. If a question is missing - write to us.

Ask us directly
Who actually sends the messages?
You do - from your business, to your customers. We prepare the copy, the timing and the plan - sending stays in your hands, so customers receive a message from the business they know. We never get access to your phone, your calendar or your systems.
Is this legal?
Messaging rules vary by country - for example the TCPA in the US, PECR and UK GDPR in the UK, and CASL in Canada - and most of them share the same foundations: prior consent or an existing relationship, clear sender identification, and a simple way to opt out. That is exactly how the process is built: we work only with past customers the business is permitted to contact, every message identifies the business clearly, and every message carries an explicit opt-out - anyone who opts out receives nothing further. We never buy lists and never contact strangers. Important: this is general background about how we work, not legal advice - your business is responsible for confirming the rules that apply in your country.
How long does it take?
Up to 7 days from the moment you share the list. You receive ready-to-send messages and a send plan for the week, and at the end of the period - a full results report.
And if no customers come back?
In the first week you paid nothing, and we don't promise a specific result. What is guaranteed: at the end of the 7 days you'll have accurate data on what happened in the field - and the decision whether to continue stays with you.
What do I need to prepare to start?
Just a simple list of past customers - first name and phone. Everything else (copywriting, timing, send plan, tracking sheet and report) is on us.
What data do you receive about my customers?
The minimum possible: first name and phone number. We don't ask for treatment history, addresses, emails or any other identifying detail. The data is used solely for your pilot and is not passed to any other party.
Do I need to give you access to my calendar or booking system?
No. There's no integration, no installation and no password sharing. You keep working with your calendar exactly as usual - we work around it.
What does the message look like? Can I see it before anything goes out?
Yes. Every version is passed to you for approval before the week begins. Nothing goes out that you haven't seen and approved, and you can ask for changes in tone, content or timing.
What happens to a customer who asks not to be contacted?
They receive no further messages - period. Every message includes an explicit opt-out, and anyone who opts out is immediately removed from the list and is included neither in the gentle reminder nor in any future contact.
What does it cost after the pilot week?
Continuation costs $65 per week (or a monthly plan), and only if you explicitly chose it after seeing the report. There's no auto-renewal, no commitment period and you can stop anytime. Full pricing.
Which languages can the messages be sent in?
The default copy is in natural, warm English. If your customer audience speaks a different language - tell us, and we'll adapt.
